# Product demo: Northwind Analytics

Client call with Dana Reyes, Head of Operations at Contoso Logistics.

Goal: leave with agreement to run a 30-day pilot.

> Breathe. Smile. Slow down on the first sentence.

---

## Opening (2 min)

- Thank Dana and the team for their time
- Confirm the time box: **45 minutes**, demo in the middle
- Ask: "Is anyone else joining us?"

State the plan in one sentence:

"I'll ask a few questions, show you how Northwind handles those exact cases, and we'll finish by agreeing on next steps."

==Do not open with features. Open with their problem.==

---

## Agenda

1. Their situation and goals (10 min)
2. Live demo, tailored to what we hear (20 min)
3. Pricing and rollout (5 min)
4. Questions and next steps (10 min)

Parking lot for anything off-topic. Write it down, promise a follow-up.

---

## Discovery questions

- What is the number one problem you want solved this quarter?
- How do you report on late deliveries today, and who reads that report?
- Who else is involved in the decision?
- What happens if nothing changes in the next six months?
- How will you measure success after 90 days?

Listen for: **time lost per week**, **who owns the pain**, **a deadline**.

*Take notes. Repeat their words back to them in the demo.*

---

## Demo flow

- [ ] Close email, chat and notifications
- [ ] Open the demo workspace in a clean browser profile
- [ ] Start with the dashboard (their top KPI)
- [ ] Show the late-delivery alert rule being created
- [ ] Drill into a single route and its timeline
- [ ] Export the weekly report to PDF
- [ ] Show the permissions page for their ops team
- [ ] Pause and ask: "Does this match how your team works?"

If the demo breaks: say "let me show you the recording of this part" and open the backup tab.

---

## Objections

**"It's too expensive"**
Compare against the cost of the problem: hours lost per week multiplied by the hourly cost. Then offer the pilot to prove it.

**"We're not ready right now"**
Ask: "What would need to happen for the timing to be right?" Agree on a date to revisit.

**"We already have a tool for this"**
Ask what they like most and what they would change. Position Northwind as the layer on top, not a rip-and-replace.

**"I need to talk to my boss"**
Offer a short summary email and a 15-minute follow-up with the decision maker.

---

## Pricing notes

- Team plan: ==$18 per user per month==, billed annually
- Pilot: ==free for 30 days==, up to 10 users, no card required
- Onboarding: one live session included, extra sessions billed separately
- Discount ceiling: 10 percent on annual billing without approval

~~Never quote the enterprise price before the security review.~~ If asked, say: "Enterprise pricing depends on your requirements, I'll get you a quote this week."

`Approval needed above 10 percent: ask the sales lead first.`

---

## Closing

1. Summarize what we agreed in **one sentence**
2. Confirm the pilot scope: team, dates, success criteria
3. Confirm owners and dates for each next step
4. Book the follow-up meeting *before* ending the call
5. Send the recap email within one hour

Thank them by name.

---

## Closing remarks (read-aloud script)

Dana, thank you again for taking the time today. I want to make sure I understood what matters most to your team: reducing the hours spent chasing late deliveries, getting one report that everyone trusts, and doing it without adding work for your dispatchers.

Based on what you showed me, the best next step is a thirty-day pilot with your northern region team. We would set up the alert rules we built together today, connect your existing delivery data in the first week, and meet every Friday to review the numbers.

If the pilot reduces late deliveries by the amount we discussed, we will have a clear case for rolling out to the other regions. If it does not, you will have lost nothing but a few hours, and you will know exactly why.

Here is what I will do on my side. Today, I will send you a short recap of this conversation, the pilot plan and a list of the information we need from your team. By Wednesday, I will have a pilot workspace ready with your branding and the dashboards you liked. On Thursday, let us do a thirty-minute kickoff with the people who will use it daily, and we can start collecting real data the following Monday.

Is there anything you would like to change in that plan, or anyone else who should be on the kickoff call? Wonderful. Thanks again, Dana, and thanks to everyone on the call. I will talk to you on Thursday.
